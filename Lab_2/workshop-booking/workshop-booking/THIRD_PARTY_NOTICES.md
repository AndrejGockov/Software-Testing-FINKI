under the Apache License, Version 2.0. Their original license headers are retained.
The license is included in `LICENSES/apache-maven-wrapper-LICENSE.txt`.

Maven downloads Spring Boot, H2 and PostgreSQL JDBC components as declared in the
POM files. Their binaries are not included in this archive.
